What Is a Land Partnership?

You contribute
the land.
We build everything else.

A land partnership is a deal structure where you bring the most foundational asset — the land — and Steel & Aire handles the rest: capital raising, project architecture, entitlements, development strategy, and lender placement.

Your land becomes your equity stake in the deal. Instead of selling your land for a one-time check, you remain a participant in the project it becomes — sharing in the returns as that land is transformed into something far more valuable.

"Most landowners sell once and watch someone else build the wealth. We offer a different path — one where your land becomes a permanent seat at the table."
How It Works

Four steps from
raw land to deal close.

01

Land Assessment

We evaluate your land — location, zoning, access, entitlement status, and highest-and-best-use potential. No cost, no commitment.

02

Strategy & Architecture

Our team designs the development thesis for your land — what to build, how to structure it, and how to maximize the value of what you already own.

03

Deal Structuring

We structure a deal where your land contribution is valued as equity — giving you a formal ownership stake and protecting your interest throughout the project lifecycle.

04

Capital & Execution

Steel & Aire raises the construction and development capital from our lender and investor network — you don't need to fund anything. We manage the raise to close.

Company Synopsis

The full picture.

A complete overview of who we are, what we do, and why we exist.

Who We Are

Steel & Aire Soluciones is a global capital and infrastructure firm headquartered in the United States, with active operations across El Salvador, Ecuador, Hawaii, Fiji, and Samoa. We are a privately held company operating at the intersection of finance, government, and infrastructure development.

Our name reflects what we build: Steel — the physical infrastructure that holds economies together — and Aire — the invisible flows of capital, data, and energy that make modern life possible. Soluciones is our commitment to solutions, not problems, wherever we operate.

Our Mission

We exist to spread wealth and abundance across the world. This is not a marketing statement — it is the organizing principle behind every deal we structure, every relationship we build, and every territory we enter. We believe that access to capital is the single most powerful lever for economic development, and that our job is to pull that lever in places and for projects where it creates the most good.

Our ultimate goal is to build a fully tokenized investment fund that makes institutional-grade infrastructure returns accessible to a global base of investors — democratizing access to the kinds of deals that have historically been reserved for the ultra-wealthy.

What We Do

Steel & Aire operates as a capital broker, fund manager, and project developer. We connect borrowers and developers with the right capital sources — drawing on our relationships with multifamily funds, family offices, private money lenders, and sovereign capital vehicles. We originate, package, and place debt and equity across four primary asset classes: nuclear energy, data centers, multifamily real estate, and tokenized fund instruments.

We also work closely with governments and diplomatic representatives in each of our operating territories — ensuring that our projects carry the sovereign alignment and regulatory clarity that institutional capital requires.

Global Footprint

Steel & Aire has physical presence and active operations in six territories: the United States (Austin TX, Salt Lake City UT, and Honolulu HI), El Salvador, Ecuador, Fiji, and Samoa. Each territory represents a strategic node in our capital and deal network — connecting local opportunity with global capital.

Our Pacific presence is particularly distinctive. With offices in Hawaii and relationships in Fiji and Samoa, we are one of the few US-based capital firms with genuine feet on the ground across the Pacific Island region — a market that is dramatically underserved by traditional finance.

Capital Network

Our lender and capital partner network includes multifamily debt funds, family offices, private money lenders, hard money lenders, sovereign development institutions, and emerging digital asset credit facilities. We maintain active relationships across all tiers of the capital stack — from first-lien senior debt to mezzanine and preferred equity.

For brokers, we operate as a co-brokerage and referral partner, giving them access to capital sources and deal types they could not access independently. For lenders, we deliver pre-packaged, fully underwritten deal flow from a pipeline that spans six countries and multiple asset classes.

The Tokenized Fund

Our most ambitious initiative is the construction of a fully tokenized investment fund — a first-of-its-kind vehicle in our market. The fund will hold real-world assets (data centers, nuclear energy projects, multifamily real estate) and represent fractional ownership through blockchain-native tokens, enabling transparent, auditable, and globally accessible investment participation.

This is not speculative crypto exposure. It is real infrastructure, financed and developed by our team, wrapped in a digital ownership structure that removes the barriers — geography, accreditation complexity, high minimums — that have historically excluded most investors from this asset class. Accredited and institutional investors are invited to engage.

Government & Diplomatic Relations

Steel & Aire works directly with government ministries, economic development agencies, and diplomatic representatives across our operating territories. Our government relationships are not peripheral to our business — they are central to it. The types of projects we develop — nuclear energy, large-scale data infrastructure, community housing — require sovereign alignment, regulatory engagement, and, in many cases, direct government participation.

We are proud to serve as a bridge between private capital markets and public development goals, structuring public-private partnerships that align investor return objectives with national infrastructure priorities.

Contact & Offices

Steel & Aire Soluciones is reachable at admin@mpeakp.com. Our US offices are located in Austin, Texas; Salt Lake City, Utah; and Honolulu, Hawaii. International operations are conducted from our teams in El Salvador, Ecuador, Fiji, and Samoa.
