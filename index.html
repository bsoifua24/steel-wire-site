Capital Structure

How we stack
a typical deal.

Every deal we structure has multiple layers of capital — each with its own return profile, risk position, and priority in the waterfall. This is what a representative structure looks like.

Senior Debt65%
First lien, lowest risk, lowest return. Banks & debt funds.
Mezzanine Debt15%
Second position. Higher yield, subordinate to senior debt.
Preferred Equity10%
Equity upside with preferred return priority. Family offices & HNW.
Common Equity / GP10%
Highest risk, highest upside. GP promote kicks in at preferred hurdle.

Percentages represent illustrative loan-to-value ratios for a $10M–$100M infrastructure deal. Actual structure varies by asset type, market, and lender mandate.

What Is a Land Partnership?

You contribute
the land.
We build everything else.

A land partnership is a deal structure where you bring the most foundational asset — the land — and Steel & Aire handles the rest: capital raising, project architecture, entitlements, development strategy, and lender placement.

Your land becomes your equity stake in the deal. Instead of selling your land for a one-time check, you remain a participant in the project it becomes — sharing in the returns as that land is transformed into something far more valuable.

"Most landowners sell once and watch someone else build the wealth. We offer a different path — one where your land becomes a permanent seat at the table."

Why Not Just Sell?

A sale gives you
one check.
A partnership gives you a future.

When you sell land outright, you receive a fixed price — and that's the end of your participation in whatever gets built. The developer captures all the appreciation, all the income, and all the long-term value.

As a land partner with Steel & Aire, your land becomes equity. You participate in the returns of the completed project — whether that's annual distributions from a rental property, proceeds from a sale at completion, or a stake in a fund built on what your land became.

Once
If you sell
One payment. No stake in what gets built. No participation in appreciation or income.
Always
As a land partner
Equity stake. Shared upside. Ongoing distributions. A seat at the table for the life of the project.
