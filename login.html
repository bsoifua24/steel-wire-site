Getting Started
This is your dedicated deal room — a private portal where you and the Steel & Aire team manage your capital raise from start to close. You can track your progress through each of the 5 phases, upload required documents, message your deal team, and see exactly what's needed at every step. Everything is organized for you; just follow the checklist.
Steel & Aire acts as your capital placement partner. We package your deal, match it to the right lenders from our network, guide you through each documentation phase, and work to get you to a term sheet and close. We don't collect anything upfront — our fee is earned at closing from deal proceeds.
Only you and the Steel & Aire admin team can see your deal room. Borrowers cannot see other borrowers' deals. Documents are stored securely in private cloud storage. Nothing is shared with lenders until you and your deal team agree that the package is ready.
That's fine — start with what you have. Phase 1 documents are the priority; you can upload later-phase documents as your raise progresses. The checklist shows you which documents are needed at which phase so you always know what to focus on next.
The Capital Raise Process
  • Phase 1 — Intake: Initial deal review. You submit your executive summary, PFS, schedule of real estate owned, and sponsor bio so we can confirm the deal is financeable.
  • Phase 2 — Soft Quote: Preliminary lender interest. We shop the deal and get a non-binding indication of rate, LTV, and loan terms from interested lenders.
  • Phase 3 — Term Sheet: Formal written loan terms. A lender issues a term sheet outlining rate, amount, fees, and conditions. Both sides negotiate before execution.
  • Phase 4 — Underwriting: Full lender due diligence. The lender orders an appraisal, reviews all financials in detail, and runs a complete credit analysis.
  • Phase 5 — Closing: Final documents, title, insurance, and funding. You close the loan and receive proceeds.
A well-prepared deal typically takes 60–120 days from intake to close. The biggest variable is how quickly and completely documents are submitted. Deals with complete, accurate documentation at every phase close fastest. The single best thing you can do is have all Phase 1 documents uploaded before your first call with us.
A soft quote is a non-binding preliminary indication of interest from a lender — it gives you a range for rate, loan-to-value, and basic terms based on an initial review. It signals that the deal is financeable but is subject to change during underwriting. Think of it as a "we're interested, here's roughly what we'd offer" from a lender.
A term sheet is a formal, written document from a lender summarizing the key loan terms they are prepared to offer. Key items to review: loan amount, interest rate (and whether it's fixed or floating), loan term, LTV/LTC ratio, origination fee, prepayment penalty, recourse vs. non-recourse, and any key conditions. A term sheet is still not a commitment — full commitment comes after underwriting.
Yes — you can upload any document at any time regardless of phase. If you already have your tax returns, appraisal, or Phase 4 documents ready, uploading them early is always a good idea. It speeds up the process when those phases arrive and shows lenders you're well-prepared.
Your Document Checklist
A PFS is a complete snapshot of your personal finances — listing all assets (cash, real estate equity, investments, retirement accounts) and all liabilities (mortgages, loans, credit cards). It must be current (within 90 days), signed, and dated. It's the first thing virtually every lender requests. Click "View Template" on the PFS checklist item for a complete fillable template.
A rent roll is a unit-by-unit breakdown of every tenant, their lease dates, contracted rent, collected rent, and unit status (occupied or vacant). It must be current — as of this month. Export it from property management software (AppFolio, Yardi, Buildium) or build it in Excel. Include all units, including vacant ones.
The T-12 is your trailing 12 months of actual income and expenses — the document lenders use to calculate Net Operating Income (NOI). It should show month-by-month columns plus a total, and must reflect actual figures — not estimates. Export from QuickBooks, AppFolio, or Yardi.
The T-12 shows what the property has done (historical actuals). The Pro Forma shows what you believe it will do at stabilized occupancy — forward-looking projections. Lenders compare both side by side. Every assumption in your Pro Forma (projected rents, vacancy rate, expense ratios) must be stated clearly and supported by market data.
Sources & Uses is a one-page breakdown of the entire capital stack — where every dollar comes from (senior debt, equity, mezzanine, etc.) and exactly where it goes (purchase price, closing costs, renovation, reserves). Sources must equal Uses to the dollar. A mismatch is one of the most common reasons a deal gets flagged immediately by a lender as unprepared.
PDF is strongly preferred for all financial documents, statements, and legal documents — it preserves formatting and prevents editing. Excel (.xlsx) is acceptable for rent rolls, T-12s, and pro formas where the lender may want to work with the numbers. Avoid screenshots or photos of documents — lenders require clean, professional presentation.
Yes — you can upload multiple files to any single checklist item. For example, if you have 2 years of tax returns as separate PDFs, upload both to the Personal Tax Returns item. All uploaded files will be listed under that item for your team to review.
Document Statuses Explained
  • Needed — Not yet uploaded, or a revision was requested. Action required from you.
  • Uploaded — File received. Your Steel & Aire team has been notified and will review it.
  • In Review — Your team is actively reviewing the document for completeness and lender-readiness.
  • Approved — Reviewed and cleared. Ready to be included in the lender package.
You'll receive an email notification whenever a document status changes.
When your team marks a document as needing revision, a red note will appear on that checklist item explaining exactly what needs to be fixed — for example, "PFS is missing your signature on page 2." Simply correct the issue and re-upload the fixed file. The status will automatically return to "Uploaded" for another review.
Once all documents for the current phase are approved, your Steel & Aire team advances your raise to the next phase — either by presenting to lenders for a soft quote, issuing a term sheet, or moving into underwriting. You'll see the phase indicator at the top of your deal room update, and your team will reach out to explain the next steps.
Capital & Terms
We place capital across all tiers of the capital stack: senior debt (first-lien mortgage loans), bridge loans, DSCR loans, construction loans, mezzanine debt, and preferred equity. For larger infrastructure projects (data centers, nuclear, industrial), we also arrange institutional equity and sovereign capital. We work with multifamily, commercial, mixed-use, and infrastructure asset types.
  • LTV (Loan-to-Value): Loan amount ÷ property value. Most lenders cap at 65–80% depending on asset type and loan type.
  • LTC (Loan-to-Cost): Loan amount ÷ total project cost. Common for construction and bridge loans.
  • DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio): NOI ÷ annual debt service. Most lenders require 1.20x–1.35x minimum.
  • NOI (Net Operating Income): Effective gross income minus operating expenses (before debt service).
  • Cap Rate: NOI ÷ property value. Used to compare properties in a market.
Steel & Aire earns a capital placement fee at closing — paid from deal proceeds, not upfront. There are no fees during the document submission phase. Exact fee amounts depend on deal size, complexity, and loan type, and will be outlined by your Steel & Aire contact before we begin marketing your deal to lenders. Nothing is charged without your prior agreement.
Recourse means that if the property doesn't generate enough to cover the loan and the lender forecloses, they can pursue you personally for any remaining deficiency — beyond just the property. Non-recourse limits the lender's remedy to the property itself. Non-recourse loans are typically only available to experienced sponsors on stabilized assets and come with "bad boy" carve-outs for fraud, misrepresentation, or environmental violations.
Working With Steel & Aire
Use the Messages section at the bottom of your deal room — your team monitors it during business hours and will respond there. For urgent matters, email admin@mpeakp.com directly with your deal name in the subject line. Do not upload documents and assume we've seen them — always send a message or email to flag something that needs immediate attention.
Document status updates typically happen within 1–3 business days of upload. Message replies are typically within 1 business day. For phase transitions and lender communications, your team will reach out proactively. If you haven't heard anything in 3+ business days after a major upload, send a message or email to follow up.
The biggest factor is document completeness. Every time a lender has to ask for something that should have been in the package, it causes delays. Before uploading, review the "View Template" guide for each document — it shows exactly what needs to be included. Complete, professional, accurately dated documents close fastest. Incomplete or sloppy submissions are the #1 reason deals stall.
